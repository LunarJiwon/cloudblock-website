CloudBlock is a Minecraft Java skyblock server. You start on a few blocks of dirt above the void and grow them into a kingdom: cobblestone generators, farms, challenges, and a leaderboard that resets every week.

Join our Discord

Java Edition 26.3 · free to play · no mods needed

Start playing in four steps

  1. Add the server

    Minecraft Java → Multiplayer → Add Server, address cloudblock.cloud.

  2. Get your island

    Type /is create. Your starter chest has lava, two ice and seeds.

  3. Build a generator

    Lava on one end of a trench, water on the other: cobblestone forms in between, forever.

  4. Climb the ranks

    Finish /challenges, sell at /shop, raise your level with /is level.

What's on your island

Upgrade your cobblestone generator through six tiers. At Diamond Age, every block can roll ore:

26 challenges

From Cobble Collector to Netherite Dream, in four tiers. Each one pays money or items, and many can be repeated.

Play together

Invite friends to your team, or join someone else's island as a co-op builder while keeping your own.

Island upgrades

Grow your border from 51×51 to 201×201 blocks, fit up to 8 teammates, and unlock better generators.

Daily rewards

Log in seven days in a row with /daily. Day seven gives $1,000 and two diamonds.

Spawn parkour

Thirteen jumps, one checkpoint and a timer. Beat the record and the whole server hears about it.

Spawn leaderboards

Top islands, richest players, playtime and parkour times, updated every minute, with statues of the top three.

Weekly island competition

Every Monday at 00:00 UTC, the three highest-level islands are paid out, and every team member gets the prize.

Questions

Which Minecraft version do I need?

Java Edition 26.3. Older Java versions can usually join too. Bedrock is not supported yet.

What happens if I fall into the void?

Nothing bad. You are sent back to your island home with your items.

Can I restart my island?

Yes, with /is reset. Your inventory, ender chest and XP are cleared too, so resets can't be farmed.

How do I report a player or get help?

Open a Support or Report ticket in our Discord. Only you and the staff can see it.
